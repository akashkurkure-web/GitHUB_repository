1. Choose how to order

Shop: ready designs (mainly home decor) have a fixed price. Add your delivery address and place the order.
Quote request: for custom parts, mass manufacturing and medical models, upload your files (up to {{ MAX_UPLOAD_MB|floatformat:0 }} MB each, or share a link for bigger files). No account is needed; we create one for you and email a link to set your password.

2. We review and quote

Our team checks your design for printability, chooses the right technology and material, and sends a quote with the price, GST and expected dispatch time. Quotes are valid for 15 days.

3. Pay to confirm

Pay by UPI or bank transfer to our company account. The account details and a UPI QR code appear on your order page. Enter the UTR number after paying; we confirm once it shows in our bank statement. Orders of ₹{{ ADVANCE_THRESHOLD }} and above need a {{ ADVANCE_PERCENT }}% advance to start, with the balance due before dispatch. We do not accept cash.

4. Production and quality check

Your parts are printed, cleaned, finished and inspected. You can follow each stage on the order tracker.

5. Dispatch and delivery

We ship by a reputed courier and share the AWB number and tracking link. Your GST tax invoice is available in your account as soon as the order ships.
