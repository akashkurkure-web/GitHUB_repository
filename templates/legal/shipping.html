  • Where we deliver: anywhere in India served by our courier partners.
  • When it ships: shop items usually ship within the lead time shown on the product (typically 3 to 7 working days). Custom and batch orders ship within the time given in your quote, counted from payment confirmation.
  • Delivery time: usually 2 to 7 days after dispatch depending on your PIN code.

  • Packing: parts are packed to protect them in transit. Please record a short unboxing video for high-value orders; it helps us settle transit-damage claims quickly.
  • Delays: if we expect a delay we will tell you with a new date. You may cancel an order that has not entered production if the new date does not work for you.
